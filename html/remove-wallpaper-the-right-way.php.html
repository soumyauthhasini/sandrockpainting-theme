How to Remove Wallpaper the Right Way

 

August 21, 2025

Wallpaper can add charm and personality to a room — but when it’s outdated or peeling, it can make your home feel stuck in the past. If you’ve ever searched for “wallpaper removal near me,” you know that removing wallpaper can be frustrating without the right tools and experience.

At Sandrock Painting, we’ve helped countless homeowners in Northeast Ohio update their spaces by removing old wallpaper and preparing the walls for a smooth, modern paint finish. Here’s how we approach the process.

Step 1 — Soak and Loosen the Wallpaper

We begin by soaking the wallpaper with a specialized wallpaper remover concentrate. This breaks down the adhesive, making it easier to peel off. In most cases, this step alone loosens the paper enough to strip it away.

Step 2 — Steam for Stubborn Areas

Sometimes, wallpaper glue is extra tough. When that happens, we use a wallpaper steamer to help lift the adhesive from the wall. This allows us to remove even the most stubborn wallpaper without damaging the surface beneath.

Step 3 — When Wallpaper Won’t Come Off

In some cases, wallpaper simply refuses to budge. Instead of risking wall damage, we take a different approach:

  • Apply a specialized primer over the wallpaper to seal it in place
  • Caulk along ceilings and trim for a seamless transition
  • Skim coat and sand the seams so that they disappear completely

When finished, you’ll never be able to tell the wall was wallpapered in the first place.

Why Professional Wallpaper Removal Matters

DIY wallpaper removal can quickly turn into a frustrating, messy job. Our professional process ensures:

  • Minimal wall damage
  • Proper surface preparation for painting
  • A clean, smooth finish that’s ready for fresh paint

By combining the right products, tools, and techniques, we make your walls look brand new again.
