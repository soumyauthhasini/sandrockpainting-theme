Our Exterior Painting Process

Exterior painting and staining projects require careful prep work to ensure that products adhere properly and last over time.

Our process typically includes:

  • Soft washing to gently remove dirt and algae
  • Pressure washing when needed
  • Treating with sodium hypochlorite to neutralize mildew
  • Scraping any loose or peeling paint
  • Caulking gaps and sealing joints
  • Priming where needed
  • Back-brushing / back-rolling when needed

Most issues with exterior paint come from poor preparation. Taking the time to properly prep surfaces is what allows the finish to hold up for years.
