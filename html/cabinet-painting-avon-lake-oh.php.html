Kitchen Cabinet Painting in Avon Lake: A Professional Process That Delivers a Factory-Smooth Finish

 

January 26, 2026

If your kitchen feels dated but your cabinets are still structurally sound, cabinet painting can be one of the smartest upgrades you make to your home. A professional cabinet repaint can completely transform the look of your kitchen — without the cost, disruption, or waste of a full cabinet replacement.

At Sandrock Painting, we specialize in professional kitchen cabinet painting, using a proven spray-applied process designed for durability, cleanliness, and a smooth, factory-like finish.

Why Cabinet Painting Is So Popular in Avon Lake Homes

Many homes in Avon Lake were built with solid wood cabinetry that still has years of life left. Rather than tearing out cabinets that function perfectly, homeowners are choosing to repaint them for a clean, modern look.

Professional cabinet painting allows you to update the style of your kitchen, improve resale appeal, avoid the mess of demolition, and get dramatic results in a shorter timeframe.

Our Cabinet Painting Process

A high-quality cabinet finish comes down to preparation, materials, and application method. Here’s how we approach every cabinet painting project:

1. Full Disassembly

We begin by removing the cabinet doors, drawer fronts, hinges and pulls. This allows us to coat every surface evenly and avoid brush marks or missed edges.

2. Dustless Sanding

All cabinet surfaces are sanded using our Festool dustless sanding system. This step promotes proper adhesion, smooths existing finishes, and keeps sanding dust out of your home. Dust control is one of the biggest advantages of professional cabinet painting.

3. Cleaning and Degreasing

After sanding, we clean all surfaces with a liquid de-glosser to remove residual sanding dust, cooking grease, oils and any other contaminants. Clean surfaces are critical for long-term durability.

4. Masking and Protection

Before spraying, we carefully mask floors, countertops, appliances, walls and surrounding surfaces. This ensures crisp lines and full protection throughout the process.

5. Professional Priming

We spray an oil-based bonding primer onto the cabinet boxes, doors and drawer fronts. Oil-based primer provides superior adhesion and creates a stable foundation for the finish coats.

6. Spray-Applied Finish Coats

We apply Sherwin-Williams Emerald Urethane using professional fine finish spray equipment for a smooth, factory-level finish, excellent durability, and resistance to wear, moisture, and cleaning. Unlike companies that brush or roll cabinet boxes, we spray the cabinet boxes as well, ensuring a consistent finish across every visible surface.

7. Reassembly

Once curing is complete, we reassemble the doors, drawer fronts, hinges and pulls. Your kitchen is left clean, functional, and completely transformed.

Why Spraying Cabinet Boxes Matters

One of the biggest differences between professional cabinet painting and lower-quality work is how the cabinet boxes are finished. Brushing or rolling cabinet boxes often leaves an “orange peel” roller texture, brush marks, and inconsistent sheen. By spraying the cabinet boxes, we achieve a uniform appearance that matches the doors and drawer fronts — something Avon Lake homeowners immediately notice.
