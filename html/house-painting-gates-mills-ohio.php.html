Professional Painting Services in Gates Mills

We provide a full range of residential painting services tailored to the homes and architecture in Gates Mills:

Interior Painting

Whether you're updating a single room or repainting your entire home, we take the time to do it right. That includes repairing stress cracks and imperfections, smoothing out problem areas, and applying clean, even coats on walls, ceilings, trim, and doors. The goal is a finished space that looks sharp and holds up over time.

Exterior Painting

Exterior painting is all about prep. We handle scraping, sanding, and addressing any failing areas before applying paint, so that the finish actually lasts. We work on a variety of surfaces including wood, aluminum, and vinyl, with a focus on durability and clean, consistent results. Our EPA certified painters keep your family safe from the potential hazards of lead-based paint.

Cabinet Painting & Refinishing

Cabinet painting is one of the best ways to transform a kitchen or bathroom without a full remodel. We use a process designed to create a smooth, factory-like finish that looks great and stands up to everyday use — not something that chips or wears away after a few months.

Wallpaper Removal & Wall Prep

Removing wallpaper the right way is the key to getting a good final result. We take care of the full process - removal, adhesive cleanup, and skim coating if needed, so that the walls are smooth and ready for paint.
