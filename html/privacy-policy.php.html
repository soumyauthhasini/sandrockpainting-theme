Privacy Policy for Sandrock Painting, Inc.

Last Updated: April 13, 2026

 

Sandrock Painting, Inc. (“Company,” “We,” “Us,” or “Our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, request a quote, or interact with our services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following information when you submit a form, request a quote, or contact us:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address (project location)
  • Any additional details you provide about your project

Usage Data

We may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Device information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide estimates and painting services
  • Communicate about your project
  • Schedule appointments
  • Send follow-ups and service-related messages
  • Improve our website and customer experience

3. Communication & Consent

By providing your contact information, you agree that Sandrock Painting, Inc. may contact you via:

  • Phone call
  • Text message (SMS)
  • Email

Your consent is required before receiving communications from us.

Data Use:

We do not share, sell, or disclose your contact information or communication preferences (including text messaging opt-in data) with any third parties.

Your information is used solely by Sandrock Painting, Inc. to provide and improve our services.

SMS Disclaimer:

Your SMS consent and phone number collected for SMS purposes will not be shared under any circumstances for marketing purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your information is used only for intended communication purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Improve website performance
  • Understand how users interact with our site

You can disable cookies in your browser settings if preferred.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We use commercially reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Maintain business records
  • Comply with legal obligations

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools and services to operate our business, such as:

  • Customer relationship management (CRM) systems
  • Scheduling and communication platforms
  • Advertising platforms (e.g., Google Ads, Google Local Services Ads)

These providers only access your information as needed to perform their services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
