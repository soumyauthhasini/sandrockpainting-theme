What to Expect From Our Interior Painting Process

A high-quality paint job starts long before the first coat goes on the wall. Our process is designed to ensure a consistent, long-lasting finish throughout your home.

  • Communication to ensure customers know when and where we’re starting
  • Protecting floors and furniture, and controlling dust
  • Repairing nail holes, stress cracks, and uneven areas
  • Large scale repair / replacement when needed
  • Re-caulking trim for clean, finished lines
  • Applying primer where necessary
  • Applying premium paint for full, even coverage

We ensure that the final result looks uniform and complete, not thin or patchy.
